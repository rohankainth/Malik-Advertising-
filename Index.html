⬡ WHO WE ARE ⬡

ABOUT MALIK ADVERTISING

Based in the heart of Paharganj, New Delhi, Malik Advertising has been transforming business frontages into brilliant visual landmarks for over 15 years. We are Delhi's most trusted premium signage studio — combining artisanal craftsmanship with modern LED and 3D technology.
From a small clinic in Connaught Place to a multi-chain retail brand in Select Citywalk, we've helped hundreds of businesses across Delhi NCR stand out with signage that speaks before you do. Our in-house team of designers, fabricators, and installation specialists ensure every sign is a masterpiece.
